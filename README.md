# CLASH // Races at the Frontline

A futuristic command-deck and first-person training prototype for the Callaso Battle concept.

## Preview

Run `node preview-server.cjs` from this folder, then open `http://127.0.0.1:4173`. No packages or build step are needed.

## Prototype features

- Command lobby with 5v5, 10v10, 15v15, 20v20, and 25v25 match-size selection.
- Training Ground: a playable first-person raycast simulation with movement, sprint, slide, jump, aim, firing, reload, target hit/accuracy counters, squad comms, and five fictional throwable-device effects.
- Operative Archive with 20 selectable species skins, including Black and White human operators.
- Arsenal with 50 original named weapon platforms: 15 assault rifles and 35 across SMG, shotgun, sniper, support, marksman, and sidearm classes.
- Social screen with active friends, dispatches, sample rooms, invite actions, and room setup.
- Reward track and local notification previews.

## Scope

This is a front-end prototype. Its online-player total, friend activity, reward progress, matchmaking, room codes, and invites are sample UI data; no accounts, multiplayer server, or live network services are connected. The training bay is a code-drawn 2.5D simulation, not a production 3D mobile game. Character and weapon cards are original prototype illustrations, not final realistic 3D assets.
